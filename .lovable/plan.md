# Buyerfront two-service homepage evolution

## Outcome
Evolve the existing one-page site so AI Visibility remains the flagship offer and AI Workflow Transformation becomes a complementary, substantial service line, without redesigning the brand or disrupting the working Snapshot flow.

## Homepage changes
- Update navigation to meaningful on-page destinations: AI Visibility, AI Workflows, How We Work, and the existing primary Snapshot action. Omit empty Insights and About destinations.
- Replace the opening copy and actions with the supplied broader positioning and route both actions to the correct sections.
- Add the concise commercial-context section with two service cards, giving AI Visibility slightly greater visual emphasis.
- Rework the existing methodology and service content into a complete AI Visibility section with Measure, Diagnose, Improve, Monitor; offer progression; and the required probabilistic-results disclaimer.
- Preserve Snapshot #001 facts and figures, but frame them as internal buyer-intent research rather than a client case study.
- Add the AI Workflow Transformation section with Discover, Prioritise, Implement, Optimise; the three-stage commercial path; example opportunity areas; and a simple contact action.
- Add shared principles and broaden the audience statement without narrowing Buyerfront to one industry.
- Update the final call to action and footer links for both services.

## Enquiries and privacy
- Keep the existing AI Visibility Snapshot form, validation, lead storage, notification, honeypot, and rate-limit logic unchanged.
- Use a simple pre-addressed email contact for workflow enquiries rather than changing the existing lead table and notification pipeline, avoiding unnecessary backend risk.
- Keep the Privacy Notice and its links intact.

## Metadata and quality checks
- Update homepage title and description while preserving the existing favicon and social image.
- Remove em dashes from public website copy.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, all section links and calls to action, Snapshot client-side validation, Privacy navigation, and the current build/runtime status.
- Confirm Snapshot backend code remains unchanged and no invented results, client claims, or guarantees are introduced.
