# Buyerfront

Build a polished, lean one-page B2B consultancy website for a new business. Use [BRAND] as a temporary visible wordmark/name because the final brand is not yet selected. Do not invent a final company name. The website's only primary conversion goal is a free AI Visibility Snapshot request.

Positioning:
Descriptor: AI Buyer Intelligence & Growth
Primary headline: “When customers ask AI what to buy, does your brand make the shortlist?”
Supporting copy: “Your customers are using ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI search to research products, compare vendors and decide what to buy. [BRAND] measures where your business appears in those buying journeys, identifies the competitors being recommended instead, and helps improve your chances of being discovered and considered.”
Primary CTA: “Get My Free AI Visibility Snapshot”
Microcopy: “No generic AI score. We test real buying questions relevant to your business.”

Visual direction:
Make this feel like a premium B2B intelligence/strategy consultancy, not a neon-purple AI startup, generic SaaS template, or social media agency. Clean, editorial, sophisticated, confident, highly legible, responsive and especially excellent on mobile. Use restrained typography, generous whitespace, subtle data/intelligence visual cues, excellent hierarchy and tasteful micro-interactions. No stock-photo people. Avoid excessive gradients, glowing AI brains, robots, circuit graphics, or generic AI imagery. Do not use em dashes anywhere in the visible copy.

Page structure:
1. HERO with descriptor, headline, supporting copy, CTA and microcopy.

2. PROOF section titled “AI is already choosing which brands buyers see.” Use our real measured CRM experiment as an illustrative case study. Heading: “We tested the AI buyer journey for CRM software.” Show four clear data points: HubSpot 38.8% share of observed citation mentions; Salesforce 14.4%; Pipedrive 13.7%; Zoho CRM 11.5%. Then heading “But HubSpot.com wasn’t the whole story.” Explain: “HubSpot’s brand appeared across a much wider evidence ecosystem, including independent publishers, communities, comparison content and other third-party sources.” Takeaway: “AI visibility isn’t simply another Google ranking.” Add a tasteful visual comparison chart/card that works beautifully on mobile. Add methodology note: “Observed September 2026 using a fixed set of CRM buyer-intent queries. AI responses are probabilistic and results can vary by engine, query and time. Citation patterns show observed associations, not proof of causation.” Do not imply this was paid client work or a client case study.

3. EXPLANATION section titled “What happens when we test your brand?” Copy: “We ask the kinds of questions your prospective customers actually ask AI.” Show sample prompts such as “What’s the best [category] for a 50-person company?”, “What are the best alternatives to [competitor]?”, “Which [category] is best for [specific use case]?”, “[Your brand] vs [competitor]: which is better for [buyer type]?”
Then show five diagnostic gaps in a clean responsive system:
01 Discovery gap: Does AI know your company belongs in the category at all?
02 Shortlist gap: Does AI know your brand but recommend competitors instead?
03 Positioning gap: Are you appearing for the buyer types and use cases you actually want to win?
04 Citation gap: AI mentions you, but relies on other sources rather than your content.
05 Evidence ecosystem gap: Competitors are reinforced by stronger independent sources, comparisons, reviews, editorial coverage or other credible evidence.

4. FREE SNAPSHOT conversion section. Heading: “See what your buyers may be seeing before they ever reach your website.” Explain that we test a focused sample of commercially relevant AI buying questions and show: whether the brand makes the shortlist; which competitors appear instead; biggest visibility gap; an important source/evidence pattern; the opportunity we would investigate first. CTA: “Test My Brand Free”. Include a concise form with Work email, Company, Website, What do you sell?, Main competitor (optional). Make form functional at UI level with validation and a clear success state. Do not add paid backend services unless needed. If no backend is configured, structure it cleanly so submission handling can be connected next without pretending data is stored.

5. METHOD section: Measure → Diagnose → Improve → Monitor.
Measure: Establish how your brand currently appears across commercially relevant AI buying journeys.
Diagnose: Investigate where competitors outperform you, what evidence supports those answers and which gaps appear realistically influenceable.
Improve: Prioritise and execute improvements across areas that matter, which may include owned content, positioning, technical accessibility, comparisons, authority and third-party presence.
Monitor: Repeat controlled measurements, track changes and continuously refine the programme.
Prominent integrity statement: “We don’t promise to rank you #1 in ChatGPT.” Supporting copy: “AI responses are probabilistic and no legitimate agency controls what an AI system recommends. We measure what happens, improve the signals we can legitimately influence and monitor the results.”

6. FAQ with these questions and concise answers:
“Is this SEO?” Answer: It overlaps with SEO, but the problem is broader. Traditional SEO focuses heavily on visibility in search results. We examine how brands are represented, compared, cited and recommended inside AI-assisted buying journeys.
“Can you guarantee my company will be recommended by ChatGPT?” Start answer with “No.” Then explain AI outputs vary and we improve influenceable factors rather than guaranteeing recommendations.
“Can’t I use an AI visibility tool myself?” Start with “Yes.” Then: Software can measure mentions, citations and competitors. Our work is focused on turning that information into commercial diagnosis, prioritised action and execution so your team doesn’t have to build and operate an AI discovery programme internally.
“Which AI platforms do you measure?” Answer: We use relevant AI discovery platforms and specialist measurement infrastructure depending on the project. Engine coverage can change as platforms and buyer behaviour evolve.
“Who is this for?” Answer: Initially, B2B SaaS and high-value businesses where being excluded from a buyer’s consideration set can mean losing a valuable customer.

7. FINAL CTA section. Heading: “Your customers are already asking AI what to buy.” Subheading: “Find out whether it’s sending them to you or your competitors.” CTA: “Get My Free AI Visibility Snapshot”. Microcopy: “Focused buyer-journey analysis. No obligation. No guaranteed-ranking nonsense.”

Navigation should be minimal: [BRAND] wordmark left, Proof, How it works, FAQ, and CTA on desktop; elegant compact mobile nav. Footer should be minimal with placeholder legal/privacy links and a clear note that final company identity/details are pending. Do not invent testimonials, client logos, customer counts, revenue claims, awards, certifications or partnerships.

SEO/meta: write sensible title/meta description around AI visibility, AEO, GEO, AI search visibility, AI buyer intelligence and B2B SaaS, without keyword stuffing. Use semantic accessible HTML and strong mobile performance.

Do not add a blog, login, dashboard, client portal, pricing calculator, chatbot, paid plans, or extra pages. Build only the conversion-focused V1.
